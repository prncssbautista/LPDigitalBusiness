2. Step-by-Step Payhip Launch Roadmap

Week 1
Product & Lead Magnet
Pick 1 core niche. Build your primary paid product (e.g., Budget Tracker) plus a $0 freebie version to capture buyer emails.
Week 2
Payhip Setup & Upload
Connect PayPal or Stripe under Settings > Payment Details. Create your store page and upload file assets (ZIP/PDF).
Week 3
High-Converting Mockups
Design 16:9 laptop/tablet mockup frames in Canva. Highlight key automated features with clear zoomed-in text cards.
Week 4
Organic Traffic Campaign
Post 3–5 Pinterest pins daily pointing to Payhip. Publish daily 15-sec TikTok/Reels screen recordings showing product features.

4. Traffic & Conversion Growth Strategies

📌 Pinterest Keyword Engine

Create 3 to 5 Pinterest pins daily using high-intent search titles (e.g., "Aesthetic Monthly Budget Planner 2026"). Link pins directly to your Payhip store for passive, long-term traffic.

🎥 Short-Form Screen Demos

Record 15-second TikTok/Reels screen captures demonstrating the problem vs. solution (e.g., "Watch me auto-calculate my monthly savings in 5 seconds"). Put your store link in bio.

🎁 Freemium Funnel Strategy

List a simplified $0 basic version of your template on Payhip. Inside the free PDF download, include a 30% OFF discount coupon code for your full paid master product.
